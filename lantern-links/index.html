A path for every little light

How to play

Drag from a numbered lantern to its matching twin. You can start at either end. Fill every tile with a path, without crossing another color.

Drag backward to erase. Grab a path midway to shorten it, or start at a lantern to redraw its color. Tap-to-draw works too: select a lantern, then tap neighboring tiles.

Keyboard: arrows explore the garden. Space selects a lantern; arrows then draw. Escape stops drawing. Z undoes the last stroke. The numbered lanterns and labeled controls identify every color.

Each new evening is generated and checked against a complete solution. No timer, lives, or limit on retries.

A little bookmark
