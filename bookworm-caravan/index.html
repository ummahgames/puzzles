A room full of possibilities

Connect at least three neighboring letters. Paths may bend; each tile can be used once. Tap letters, drag across them, or use Tab and Enter/Space. Backtrack by selecting the previous letter. Press Enter outside a tile to submit; Escape clears.

Words disappear, letters fall, and fresh letters arrive. Words of five or more letters create reward tiles. Longer words and rare letters score more.

♨ Clear an ember by including it in a word. Each valid word advances embers down one space, as well as any fall caused by cleared tiles. At the bottom you have one final turn to use it. Invalid words do not advance time.

Journey stages require a score, a number of words, and sometimes a commission. All destinations are open to explore. Endless continues until an ember breaches the shelf. Quiet Study has no lethal hazards.

Hints reveal a common word and mark the run assisted. Rearranging creates a fresh board and extinguishes embers; it also marks assistance. Assisted completions still count. The bundled historical dictionary accepts uncommon words, but generation and hints use a smaller familiar vocabulary.

Save codes preserve the complete run and atlas. Copy one before closing; progress also stays in the address fragment. Old Bookworm Caravan codes belong to the previous edition and are not imported here.
